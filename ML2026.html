M 375T — Mathematical Foundations of Machine Learning — Fall 2026

  • M 375T, Unique Number 59335 — Tuesday & Thursday, 3:30–5:00 PM, PMA 5.122
  • Instructor: Matias G. Delgadino — matias.delgadino@utexas.edu
  • The complete syllabus (grading, office hours, university policies) is on the course Canvas site.

References

  • Calder, J., & Olver, P. J. (2025). Linear Algebra, Data Science, and Machine Learning. Springer. (Primary reference, cited as C&O below)
  • Gordan Zitkovic. Lecture notes for “Introduction to Stochastic Processes”. Zitkovic lecture notes
  • Cover, T. M., & Thomas, J. A. (2006). Elements of Information Theory (2nd ed.). Wiley. (Entropy and relative entropy: Ch. 2; Sanov's theorem via the method of types: §11.1–11.4. Cited as C&T below)
  • MacKay, D. J. C. (2003). Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms. Cambridge University Press. Free PDF from the author's site
  • Shalev-Shwartz, S., & Ben-David, S. (2014). Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms. Cambridge University Press.
  • Strang, G. (2016). Introduction to Linear Algebra. Wellesley-Cambridge Press.
